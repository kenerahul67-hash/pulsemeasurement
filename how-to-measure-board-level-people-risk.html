How To Measure Board-Level People Risk

Boards need current people risk, not a manually assembled reassurance deck built from stale survey averages and lagging attrition numbers.

To measure board-level people risk, combine live signals on retention, capacity, alignment, blockers, leadership follow-through, and action status into a governed organisational health view.

Board-Level Signals

Retention risk

Where critical people or teams show sustained drift.

Capacity risk

Where overload threatens delivery or wellbeing.

Strategy alignment

Whether priorities are reaching team-level work.

Follow-through

Whether known risks are being acted on.

FAQ

What people risk should boards see?

Retention risk, capacity strain, leadership follow-through, strategy alignment, blockers, culture risk, and whether action is being taken.
